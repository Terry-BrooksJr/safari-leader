# Top KPI cards (at-a-glance, today)
These are the numbers a facility director checks first thing every morning:

- [ ] Children present right now — checked-in count vs. expected today (e.g. 42 / 50 checked in)
- [ ] Staff-to-child ratio — current ratio vs. the required ratio, ideally per room/age group. This is the single most important childcare metric because it's a licensing/compliance requirement. Flag red when out of compliance.
- [ ] Capacity utilization — enrolled vs. licensed capacity (e.g. 88% full)
- [ ] Staff on site — present vs. scheduled today, with coverage gaps highlighted
- [ ] Absences today — children marked absent, plus unexplained/no-shows


## Attendance widgets

- [] Live check-in/check-out feed (who's arrived, who's been picked up, who's still expected)
- [] Attendance rate trend (this week vs. last week, or 30-day line)
- [] Late pickups 
- [] late arrivals count


## Enrollment widgets

- [] Active enrollments + new this month (trend)
- [] Open slots / availability by program or age group — drives revenue decisions
- [] Waitlist count and pending applications needing review


## Staffing & compliance widgets

- [ ] Open shifts / scheduling gaps for the next 7 days
- [ ] Credentials expiring soon — CPR, first aid, background checks (childcare staff have hard regulatory expirations; this is high-value and easy to overlook)
- [ ] Instructor schedule for today (ties to your ChildSchedule.instructor field)
 

## Children / safety widgets

- [ ] Allergy & medical-alert roster for kids present today (safety-critical)
- [ ] Immunization records missing or expiring
- [ ] Incident reports filed today
- [ ] Birthdays this week (nice-to-have, parent-engagement touch)
- [ ] Communication widgets
- [ ] Unread parent messages / notifications needing action (you have a communication app with notifications)
- [ ] Recent announcements sent
